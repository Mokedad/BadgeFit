# BadgeFit on the job: Probationary Constables and Constables (plan, 18 November 2026)

**Status: a plan, not built. Waiting for the owner to approve it or change it.** Nothing here
should be built until the owner says so. The mockup it came from is the third screen of the
"Academy prep, TrueCoach, on the job" sketch shown on 18 November 2026.

## Why this stage matters

BadgeFit's members leave once they pass, the way people leave a dating app once they meet
someone. The last stage keeps them: people who are already on the job are the ones applicants
trust most, and helping the next group pass gives them a reason to stay.

The path is: PCT prep (Prep Pass), Academy (the pass carries on), On the job (this plan).

## Two roles

### Mentor (free)

- Anyone who has passed the PCT can answer questions in Community (the area "Ask" posts already
  exist, with "best answer").
- A member becomes a Mentor when their answers are picked as best: 3 best answers to start, then
  one a month to keep it. A star shows on their Hayden and beside their name.
- While they are a Mentor the Prep Pass is free for them (Pass days, see
  BADGEFIT_MONETISATION.md). Only another member can pick a best answer, so it cannot be gamed by
  typing in your own numbers.
- Answers stay in the open: one answer helps everyone who reads it. No private coaching chats
  (the owner's own experience: a coach in Messenger with no time for anyone).

### Creator (later, paid)

- A Probationary Constable or Constable builds a plan in BadgeFit for a job they know, for example
  the selection for a specialist unit (tactical, public order, dog or water police), a return to
  fitness after an injury, or training around shift work.
- Hayden runs the plan day by day for whoever buys it: cues, countdown, check-ins. That is the
  pitch to creators: a PDF is dropped in week two, a coached plan gets finished and recommended.
- Buyers pay once in the app. The creator keeps most of each sale; BadgeFit and Apple take a cut
  (Apple 15% in its small business programme; BadgeFit's share to be decided).
- Week 1 can be free to try. A plan page shows who made it, how many follow it and what a week
  looks like.
- "Share to Instagram": a picture card of the plan with a link, so creators bring people in
  themselves.

## What people see

- Stage on their page: "On the job". Never a rank, a badge number or a station: officers often
  have rules about saying they are police online, and "On the job" keeps them safe.
- No police insignia, logos or uniform pictures anywhere (BadgeFit is not affiliated with any
  police service).
- A creator's plan card: name, Mentor star if they have one, the plan's title, weeks and days a
  week, a few exercise pictures, the price, "Week 1 free" and "Get plan".

## Before any of it is built

1. **Employer approval.** Paid side work usually needs the employer's OK. A creator confirms they
   have it before their first plan goes on sale; BadgeFit does not ask which service they work for.
2. **Plans are checked** before they go up: sensible volume, no injury risk, no promise of passing
   a selection. A refund rule for plans that are not what they say.
3. **Licences.** The exercise pictures are licensed to openGym only, and the code is a fork of
   openGym (AGPL). Selling anything needs BadgeFit's own media licence and the original author's
   agreement (see BADGEFIT_MONETISATION.md).
4. **Payments.** Apple in-app purchase for buyers; a payments provider that pays creators, with
   GST and invoices; terms for creators and buyers.
5. **People first.** Mentors before creators: a shop needs buyers, and Mentors are what bring the
   first ones.

## Order

1. Mentor star and Pass days for best answers (after payments exist, since the reward is Pass days).
2. Creator plans for a few invited creators, the owner first.
3. Open to every Mentor who wants to sell.
