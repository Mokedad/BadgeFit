# TrueCoach schedule, owner's sample from earlier in 2026

Read from a screenshot the owner sent on 18 November 2026. A sample of the coach's style, not the
March 2027 Academy plan. Not built into the app yet. Cells marked (check) were hard to read.

16 weeks, three days a week: Monday, Wednesday (from week 6), Friday. Week 1 has nothing; week 2
starts on the Friday. Week 14 is empty (rest week). Week 16 has Monday and Wednesday only (test week).

## Every training day ends with

Foam roll (back, ITB, quads), thread the needle, glute bridge 2 x 10.

## Wednesday (weeks 6-13, 15-16): recovery

Recovery jog at 70% effort, 15 to 30 minutes ("medium, not too easy, not too hard").

## Compared with the BadgeFit Academy prep plan (built 18 November 2026)

- TrueCoach: 3 days a week, body weight only, push-ups climb from 10s to 25s, intervals grow from
  200 m to 800 m, a rest week (14) and a light test week (16).
- BadgeFit Academy: 6 days a week, gym lifts plus a circuit, steady and long runs, re-tests in
  weeks 4 and 8.
