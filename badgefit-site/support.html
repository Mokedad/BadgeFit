BadgeFit Support

BadgeFit helps you train for a police fitness test: a plan to your test date, the six standards, runs with Hayden, and a crew testing when you do.

Get in touch

Email hello@badgefit.com.au. We answer within two working days.

Common questions

  • Delete my account: Settings, Account, Delete account. Your account and training are removed from the server.
  • Hayden cannot hear me: hands-free listening needs headphones. Without them, tap Hayden and speak.
  • Leave my crew: open the crew from Home and tap Leave the crew. Come back from Settings.

Please note

BadgeFit is not affiliated with any police service. Check your service's current standards, and see a doctor before starting a new programme.
