BadgeFit Privacy Policy

Last updated 13 November 2026

BadgeFit is a training app for people preparing for a police fitness test. This page says what the app keeps, why, and what you can do about it.

What we keep

  • Your account: the name you choose, and either a password (stored only as a one-way hash) or the fact that you sign in with Google or Apple. From Google or Apple we receive an identifier, your name and your email address, used only to sign you in.
  • Your training: your plan, workouts, runs, body weight, measurements and test results, so they are there on every device you sign in on.
  • Run routes: if you switch GPS on for a run, the route of your last 10 runs is kept with your training. GPS is off unless you turn it on.
  • Reminders: if you allow notifications, the address your phone gives us to deliver them.

Guests

If you use BadgeFit without an account, your training stays on your own device and is not saved on our server. To limit guests to a number of coach questions a day we keep a random guest number and a count for the day, nothing else.

The coach (Hayden) and AI

When you ask Hayden a question, or ask for a plan or a review, the question and the training details needed to answer it are sent to an AI service (Google Gemini) to write the answer and to produce Hayden's voice. The app asks for your go-ahead before the first time. We do not send your name, email address or password.

Apple Health and Health Connect

If you switch this on, BadgeFit writes your sessions to Apple Health or Health Connect and can read heart rate and body weight from it. That data stays on your phone and in your BadgeFit training log. It is never used for advertising and never sold.

What we do not do

  • No advertising, no trackers and no analytics services.
  • We do not sell or rent your information to anyone.

Where it is kept

On a server we rent in Australia, with a nightly backup. Connections to it are encrypted.

Your choices

  • Delete your account: Settings, Account, Delete account, in the app or on the website. This removes your account and your training from the server.
  • Switch things off: GPS, notifications, Apple Health and the AI coach can each be turned off.

Children

BadgeFit is for adults preparing for a police fitness test and is not directed at children under 16.

Changes and contact

If this policy changes, the date at the top changes with it. Questions: hello@badgefit.com.au.
